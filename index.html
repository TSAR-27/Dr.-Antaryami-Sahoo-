Overview

Prof. Dr. Antaryami Sahoo is a dermatologist and Professor of Skin, V.D. & Leprosy. He holds an M.D. in Skin & V.D. and has practised for more than 49 years, treating conditions of the skin, hair and nails, as well as leprosy and venereal diseases.

Patients are seen in his Skin Care OPD, where each visit ends with a clear diagnosis and a treatment plan.
